01 Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how SYNERBIT (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED (“Synerbit”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on our corporate website, synerbit.in (the “Website”). This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

02 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help websites operate, remember information, understand how visitors use the website, and improve website performance and user experience.

03 Why Synerbit Uses Cookies

Synerbit uses cookies and similar technologies primarily to understand how visitors interact with the Website and to measure website traffic and performance:

  • Understand website traffic and visitor activity.
  • Measure website usage and engagement.
  • Understand general trends in how visitors use the Website.
  • Improve the Website and its content based on usage information.

04 Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (“Google Analytics”), a web analytics service provided by Google, to understand how visitors use our Website. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and similar technologies to distinguish users and maintain session information.

05 Google Analytics Cookies

Cookie Purpose Typical Default Duration Type
_ga Used to distinguish users and help measure website usage. Up to 2 years Analytics
_ga_<container-id> Used to persist session state for Google Analytics. Up to 2 years Analytics

Note: Actual cookie duration may be reduced by browser restrictions or other technical factors.

06 Information Collected Through Google Analytics

Depending on the configuration of Google Analytics and applicable consent settings, analytics may collect information such as the number of users, session statistics, approximate location, and browser or device information. Analytics information is used to understand Website usage and improve our online presence.

07 Consent and Analytics Cookies

Where required by applicable law, we will seek appropriate consent before using non-essential analytics cookies. Our Website may use a cookie consent mechanism that allows visitors to accept or reject analytics cookies.

Where Google Consent Mode is implemented, Google tags can adjust their behavior according to the visitor’s consent choices. The analytics_storage setting controls whether analytics cookies may be stored.

08 Essential Technologies

The Website may use technologies that are necessary for basic website operation, security, or delivery of requested functionality. Such technologies are not used for advertising purposes unless specifically stated in this Policy.

09 Third-Party Services

Google Analytics is a third-party analytics service. Information collected through Google Analytics may be processed by Google in accordance with Google's applicable policies and terms.

If we add other third-party services that use cookies or similar tracking technologies—such as advertising platforms, embedded media, social media tools, reCAPTCHA, or other analytics services—we may update this Cookie Policy to describe the relevant technologies.

10 Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block cookies, delete existing cookies, or receive a notification before cookies are stored. Disabling cookies may affect certain website functionality.

11 Google Analytics Opt-Out

You may use available Google tools or browser settings to limit or opt out of certain Google Analytics measurement technologies. Availability and functionality of opt-out mechanisms may change over time.

12 No Sale of Personal Information

Synerbit does not sell personal information collected through cookies for monetary consideration. Analytics information is used for website measurement, improvement, security, and related business purposes.

13 Relationship With Our Privacy Policy

This Cookie Policy forms part of our broader privacy practices. Our Privacy Policy explains how Synerbit handles personal information and other information that may be collected through the Website.

14 Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Website, analytics configuration, technologies, legal requirements, or business practices. The “Last Updated” date indicates when it was most recently revised.
